About Us

GRIDS was established in 2017 as the first graduate organization dedicated to Data Science within USC Viterbi’s Thomas Lord Department of Computer Science. It is now the largest Data Science community at USC, boasting a robust and ever-growing alumni network

Mission Statement

Advance the academic and professional interests of Informatics, Data Science, and Computer Science students through experiential learning, corporate partnerships, and networking opportunities.

Objectives

Build a Thriving Community

Create the strongest interdisciplinary AI & Data Science network at USC.

Experiential Learning

Hands-on semester projects in AI, ML, NLP, Computer Vision, and Data Engineering.

Industry Partnerships

Bridge academia and industry through workshops, sponsorships, and recruiting pipelines.

Elevate Career Outcomes

Prepare GRIDS members for top roles in AI and data science.
